# 11Hunt

### Find opportunities. Build solutions. Get clients.

11Hunt is an AI-powered opportunity discovery platform designed to help beginners, freelancers, and student entrepreneurs find real business opportunities and turn them into actionable solutions.

## The Problem

Many beginners can build websites, AI tools, automations, or digital products — but struggle with the hardest part:

**Finding someone who actually needs what they can build.**

11Hunt explores the journey from discovering a potential business opportunity to building and reaching out with a relevant solution.

## Core Workflow

**Find → Analyze → Opportunity → Build → Outreach → Client**

The platform uses an evidence-first approach to help distinguish between:

- **FACT** — directly observed evidence
- **INFERENCE** — reasonable conclusion from evidence
- **ASSUMPTION** — something that still needs validation
- **UNKNOWN** — information that is not currently known

This helps prevent assumptions from being presented as confirmed business problems.

## Key Features

- AI-guided opportunity discovery
- Business and opportunity analysis
- Evidence-based problem identification
- Opportunity cards and recommendations
- Chief AI assistant for guided actions
- Structured opportunity pipeline
- Outreach preparation
- Human approval before important actions
